## 1. Bloque Elemental / Pre-Intermediate (Ítems 1–25)

| # | Frase completada con la opción correcta | Opción | Punto gramatical clave |
|---|---|:---:|---|
| 0 | *My sister and I **like** going to the movies.* | **A** | `like + -ing` |
| 1 | *Oh, he’s **in** London today.* | **A** | Preposición de lugar (`in` + ciudad/país) |
| 2 | *No, but there **are** a lot of other animals I like.* | **C** | `There are` + plural |
| 3 | *Where **is** Alicia from?* | **A** | Present Simple `to be` |
| 4 | *My mother and father **are** both very tall.* | **C** | Sujeto plural + `are` |
| 5 | *Oh no, it’s my **mother’s** old dining table.* | **C** | Genitivo sajón singular (`'s`) |
| 6 | *No, I **never** go out.* | **B** | Adverbio de frecuencia negativo tras *No* |
| 7 | *No, she **isn’t**. She’s not well today.* | **A** | Respuesta corta con `to be` |
| 8 | ***How many** eggs do we have?* | **C** | Contable plural (`How many`) |
| 9 | ***Take** the 236. It stops outside.* | **A** | Imperativo para dar indicaciones |
| 10 | *No, Mom can’t find **her** hat.* | **C** | Adjetivo posesivo femenino (`her`) |
| 11 | *Yes, I’d like to buy **these** pants.* | **D** | Demostrativo plural (`these` + *pants*) |
| 12 | *Oh no! The cat **is eating** it.* | **C** | Present Continuous (acción ocurriendo ahora) |
| 13 | *It’s **more** comfortable than the other one we had.* | **D** | Comparativo de adjetivo largo (`more ... than`) |
| 14 | *Could you get **some** milk, please?* | **C** | `some` en peticiones educadas (*Could you...?*) |
| 15 | *I **’d like** to play tomorrow…* | **D** | `would like + to infinitive` |
| 16 | *I **played** tennis with my friend on Saturday.* | **B** | Past Simple (*last Saturday*) |
| 17 | *I’m **going to** buy some this afternoon.* | **D** | `be going to + infinitive` (intención) |
| 18 | *Where **were** you last Tuesday?* | **A** | Past Simple `to be` con *you* |
| 19 | *…but I **have** to take my daughter to the airport.* | **C** | Obligación presente/futura (`have to`) |
| 20 | *Because it’s **the best** way to stay healthy.* | **C** | Superlativo (`the best`) |
| 21 | *I know **a lot** of people in this town.* | **B** | Cuantificador (`a lot of`) |
| 22 | *I **was** studying for that test we had on Monday.* | **A** | Past Continuous (`I was + -ing`) |
| 23 | *Yes, look! It **is going to** eat the bread…* | **D** | Predicción con evidencia visual (`be going to`) |
| 24 | *How long **have you been** married?* | **C** | Present Perfect para estados que continúan |
| 25 | *Have you **seen** that new movie yet?* | **C** | Present Perfect (`Have you + participio`) |

## 2. Bloque Intermediate / B1–B2 (Ítems 26–50)

| # | Frase completada con la opción correcta | Opción | Punto gramatical clave |
|---|---|:---:|---|
| 26 | *I’m getting in the way, **aren’t** I?* | **B** | Question tag de *I am* → **`aren't I?`** |
| 27 | *Yes, musicians **are paid** a lot of money…* | **C** | Voz pasiva en Present Simple (`are paid`) |
| 28 | *…if we buy one you **will** have to practice…* | **A** | First Conditional (`If + Present, will + inf`) |
| 29 | *No I don’t, because it’s **too** strong.* | **A** | `too + adjetivo` (exceso) |
| 30 | *…one of **the most recent** entertainment magazines…* | **C** | Superlativo (`the most recent`) |
| 31 | *I **would** buy one if we had the money…* | **D** | Second Conditional (`would + inf ... if + Past`) |
| 32 | ***Have** you packed your suitcase yet?* | **B** | Present Perfect con `yet` |
| 33 | *Well, I **could** play when I was younger…* | **C** | Habilidad general en el pasado (`could`) |
| 34 | *He asked me what I **had eaten** for the last two days.* | **B** | Reported speech / Past Perfect |
| 35 | *…said she **would** be a little late.* | **D** | Estilo indirecto (`will` → `would`) |
| 36 | *It **has been raining** since Monday.* | **B** | Present Perfect Continuous con `since` |
| 37 | *I **will** probably choose something bright…* | **A** | `will + probably` |
| 38 | *If I **could** choose, I’d probably travel…* | **C** | Second Conditional (`If I could...`) |
| 39 | *You **should** have come.* | **D** | Crítica/lástima sobre el pasado (`should have + pp`) |
| 40 | *OK, let’s go sightseeing, **shall** we?* | **B** | Question tag de `Let's` → **`shall we?`** |
| 41 | *That **can’t** be right! I don’t think that’s true.* | **B** | Deducción lógica negativa (`can't be`) |
| 42 | *What would you have done if you **had been** in my position?* | **C** | Third Conditional (`if + Past Perfect`) |
| 43 | *Maybe you should take it **back** and ask for a refund.* | **D** | Phrasal verb `take back` (devolver a la tienda) |
| 44 | *By this time tomorrow they **will be sunbathing**…* | **C** | Future Continuous (acción en progreso en el futuro) |
| 45 | *I just wish I **had chosen** an aisle seat…* | **A** | Arrepentimiento pasado (`wish + Past Perfect`) |
| 46 | *The original, **which** is a portrait…, sold for $4 million!* | **B** | Non-defining relative clause para cosas (`which`) |
| 47 | ***Although** he was almost 90…, his paintings are beautiful.* | **C** | Conector concesivo (`Although` + sujeto + verbo) |
| 48 | *Well, I **have been** looking for a new pair of boots for weeks…* | **D** | Present Perfect Continuous (`have been -ing`) |
| 49 | *…just enough to **get by**.* | **A** | Phrasal verb `get by` (apañárselas económicamente) |
| 50 | *In April next year I **will have been living** here for ten years…* | **D** | Future Perfect Continuous (duración hasta hito futuro) |

## 3. Bloque Upper-Intermediate / Advanced B2–C1 (Ítems 51–70)

| # | Frase completada con la opción correcta | Opción | Punto gramatical clave |
|---|---|:---:|---|
| 51 | *When they finally succeeded, they **had been working** on it for about 30 years.* | **B** | Past Perfect Continuous (duración previa a un pasado) |
| 52 | *I just wish the rain **would stop**.* | **A** | `wish + would` (queja o deseo de que algo cambie) |
| 53 | *…a tribe **whose** descendants live in Lima…* | **C** | Relativo posesivo (`whose`) |
| 54 | *When I was young we **would go** there every Saturday…* | **B** | Hábito en el pasado (`would + infinitive`) |
| 55 | *…if you hadn’t sat next to her…, you **wouldn’t be** so close now.* | **B** | Mixed Conditional (Pasado → Presente: `If + Past Perf, would + inf`) |
| 56 | *…the President was **forced** to resign today.* | **D** | Colocación pasiva `be forced to do something` |
| 57 | *It **might** be a good idea to check the acid levels…* | **C** | Sugerencia prudente (`It might be a good idea`) |
| 58 | *We **didn’t need to** have rushed… / We needn't have…* | **A** | Ausencia de necesidad en el pasado |
| 59 | *…until then he **had been using** a typewriter!* | **D** | Past Perfect Continuous (`until then`) |
| 60 | *If you **had booked** the ticket sooner, you’d have found a seat.* | **A** | Third Conditional (`If + Past Perfect`) |
| 61 | *You **should have** taken a taxi to the hotel…* | **B** | Consejo sobre el pasado (`should have + participio`) |
| 62 | *…they **will have seen** all the candidates by next Friday.* | **D** | Future Perfect (`by next Friday`) |
| 63 | ***However** hard I try, I can’t run any faster.* | **C** | `However + adjetivo/adverbio` (por muy duro que…) |
| 64 | *…it was lost for years and **had to be restored**.* | **B** | Obligación pasada en pasiva (`had to be + participio`) |
| 65 | ***Little** did I imagine I would ever actually meet him.* | **D** | Inversión negativa enfática (`Little did I imagine…`) |
| 66 | *I wish I **had** listened to the weather forecast.* | **A** | `wish + Past Perfect` (*had listened*) |
| 67 | *He told me he **had** been given the wrong directions.* | **B** | Past Perfect Passive en estilo indirecto (`had been given`) |
| 68 | *Once **we’ve switched** to these new cat biscuits, they won’t want…* | **A** | `Once + Present Perfect` con valor futuro |
| 69 | *Only once **did he find** he wasn’t sufficiently prepared…* | **C** | Inversión tras `Only once` (`did he find`) |
| 70 | *Well, I did say you **shouldn’t have** eaten that chicken last night.* | **D** | Reproche pasado (`shouldn't have + participio`) |
